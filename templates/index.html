How it works

  1. Draw a single digit (0-9) in the canvas
  2. Click "Predict" to analyze your drawing
  3. The AI will recognize the digit with confidence scores
  4. Click "Clear" to try another digit

Tips for best results

  • Draw the digit large and centered
  • Use smooth, continuous strokes
  • Make the digit fill most of the canvas
  • Avoid very thin or very thick lines

About the model

This classifier uses a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) trained on the MNIST dataset with over 98% accuracy.
